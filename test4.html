Carla: Hey Marcus, you look worried. Is everything okay?

Marcus: Not really. I'm short of cash this month. My roommate moved out, so now I have to pay all the rent alone. I used to share the bills with him, but now I can't afford it.

Carla: That's tough. Maybe you should find someone else to share the apartment with you. Have you thought about it?

Marcus: I don't mind sharing, but the apartment has become too crowded with all his old furniture. I need to leave some of it behind or tie everything up and move it out. Unfortunately, I haven't made up my mind yet.

Carla: You should pay attention to the deadlines — the landlord told me the rent is due on Friday. Fortunately, my brother is looking for a place. He's reliable and he probably won't owe you anything.

Marcus: That sounds great! Tell him to call me. By the way, did you hear about Diana? She got engaged last week!

Carla: Yes! She's married to the most wonderful man. I'm so happy for them both.
