Dialogue — A Salary Conversation

Bianca: Hi Marcos, thanks for coming in. We reviewed your compensation last quarter.

Marcos: Thanks, Bianca. I actually wanted to discuss my salary expectations.

Bianca: Sure. We benchmarked your role against the market, and your base salary is slightly below the market rate.

Marcos: I thought so. I've worked here for three years and I took on more responsibility last year.

Bianca: That's true. We can offer a merit increase of eight percent, effective next month.

Marcos: I appreciate that. What about my vacation balance? I still have twelve days left this year.

Bianca: You can carry over up to ten days, so make sure to schedule some time off before December.

Marcos: Understood. One more thing — I worked overtime last week. Will that be compensated?

Bianca: Yes, overtime pay was already added to your next payslip.

Marcos: Great, thank you for clarifying everything.
