Topic: A Trip I Would Like to Take
Write 6–8 sentences about a trip you would like to take. Where would you go? What would you do there? If you could go anywhere in the world, where would it be and why? Use the Second Conditional and travel vocabulary.
Estruturas esperadas
  • Second Conditional — If I had enough money, I would... / If I could travel anywhere, I would go to...
  • Passive Voice — The castle was built in... / The food is made fresh every day...
  • Modals — I should visit... / I might go to... / I could stay at a hotel...
  • Relative Clauses — a place that / a city which / the people who...
  • Travel Vocabulary — suitcase, book (a hotel), tourist, flight, castle, restaurant...
