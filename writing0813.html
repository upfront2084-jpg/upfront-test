Oral Questions — Perguntas para a Prova Oral
Faça as perguntas ao aluno e anote observacoes no campo abaixo de cada uma
Pergunta 1 — Class 40 · Struggle & Perseverance
Have you ever struggled to succeed at something difficult? Did you give up or did you keep going? What helped you make it in the end?
Foco: struggle, give up, make it, ever since, have a gift for — verbos de progresso pessoal
Pergunta 2 — Class 41 · Expressions
Is there something you do that seems hard to others but is a piece of cake for you? Have you ever carried out a project that turned out better than you expected?
Foco: piece of cake, carry out, work out, as well as, whatever / wherever — whatever-compounds
Pergunta 3 — Class 42 · Causative & Needs
Is there anything in your home or workplace that is out of order or needs fixing? Have you had it repaired recently, or are you still figuring out what to do?
Foco: have something done (causative), needs + v-ing, out of order, figure out, in need of repair
Pergunta 4 — Class 43 · Conditionals & Reporting Verbs
Can you describe a situation where you assumed something and were completely wrong? What would you do differently if you could go back? Use "if" in your answer.
Foco: assume / suppose / pretend + First & Second Conditional, as far as I know, in that case, unless
Pergunta 5 — Class 44 · Deny & Chance
Have you ever had to give someone a second chance after they denied making a mistake? How did you decide whether to trust them again?
Foco: deny + v-ing, cheat, give a chance / take the chance / miss the chance, hang up, lock up
Pergunta 6 — Class 45 · Crime & Free Production
Do you think evidence is always enough to accuse someone of a crime? What does it mean to catch someone red-handed? Tell me about a time when you had to keep an eye on something important.
Foco: accuse of, evidence, guilty / innocent, catch red-handed, get away with, keep an eye on, watch out for
