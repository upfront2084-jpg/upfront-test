Dialog — A Busy Week

Ana: Hi Carlos! What time do you usually start to work?

Carlos: I start at 8am. I go by car because I live far from the office. Does your colleague still work in the countryside?

Ana: Yes, she does. She comes from Curitiba but she doesn't have a car yet. She usually goes on foot or by bus.

Carlos: I have a meeting at 2pm and a celebration after. I don't have time to have lunch with my boss today.

Ana: Do you want to eat something now? I have a lot of food — rice, beans, salad and even dessert!

Carlos: Yes please! I already understand that you always have something good to eat!

Topic: My Daily Routine
Write 5–8 sentences about your daily routine. Include: what time you wake up, how you go to work/school, what you usually eat, and what you do after work.
Use:
  • AT + time (I wake up at...)
  • BY car / ON foot / BY plane
  • UNTIL (I work until...)
  • BEFORE / AFTER + -ing
  • USUALLY / ALREADY / STILL
