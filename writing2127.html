✏️ Topic: Comparing Things & Life Changes
Write 6–8 sentences comparing two things you own or know (appliances, accessories, places, etc.) AND describe something that has changed in your life recently. Use comparatives, superlatives, and the present perfect.
Estruturas esperadas
  • Comparative — My new phone is better than my old one / It's more expensive…
  • Superlative — This is the most wonderful place I've been to…
  • Present Perfect — I've saved money lately / I've been working a lot since…
  • Used to — I used to pay more / There used to be a market here…
  • Fortunately / Unfortunately / Probably — Unfortunately, I can't…
