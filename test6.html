Lucas's Surprise Party for Beatriz

Lucas has been planning a surprise party for his best friend Beatriz for two weeks. The party will be held at Lucas's apartment, which was decorated by his sister the day before. Beatriz has no idea about the party — Lucas told everyone to keep it a secret.

Lucas booked a cake from a bakery that is known for its amazing desserts. The cake was made with chocolate and strawberries, which are Beatriz's favorites. Lucas said: "If everything goes well, she will love it."

Lucas's friend Marco told him that Beatriz might arrive late because she had an appointment in the afternoon. Lucas replied: "If she arrives late, we will wait for her outside."

When Beatriz finally arrived, she was completely surprised. "I can't believe you did all this for me!" she said. Lucas smiled and told her that she deserved it. It was the best party he had ever organized.
