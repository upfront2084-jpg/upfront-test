✏️ Topic: Describe a Person You Know Well
Write 5–8 sentences describing a person you know well — a friend, family member, or colleague. Include their appearance, personality, and what they do.
Estruturas para usar
  • HE/SHE HAS + noun — She has long brown hair…
  • HE/SHE IS + adjective — He is very friendly and hard-working…
  • WORKS AS / IS A — She works as a teacher…
  • LIKES / LOVES / HATES + -ing — He loves playing football…
  • BOTH / NEITHER / EITHER — We both like traveling…
