Dialogue — A New Hire's First Week

Marina: Good morning, Diego! Welcome to the team. How was your first day?

Diego: Good morning! It was great, thanks. I already received my welcome kit and my badge.

Marina: Perfect. Did you meet your mentor?

Diego: Yes, I did. She showed me around and introduced me to my colleagues in the Marketing department.

Marina: Excellent. Please remember to complete your onboarding checklist by Friday. It includes your paperwork and the employee handbook.

Diego: Sure. One question — when does my probation period end?

Marina: It ends after ninety days. After that, HR will schedule a short review meeting.

Diego: Got it. And when do I get access to the payroll system to check my payslip?

Marina: You'll get access next Monday, once your contract is fully processed.

Diego: Perfect. Thank you for the overview!
