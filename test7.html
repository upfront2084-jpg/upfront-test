Questions 21–25 · Read the text and answer
The Comeback of BrightPath Solutions

Lucas had been working at BrightPath Solutions for five years when the company started to fail. The startup had succeeded in attracting big clients in its early days, but after a series of bad decisions, everything began to fall apart. Sales dropped, key employees were out of a job, and the remaining team struggled to stay motivated.

Lucas didn't give up. Ever since he became project manager, he worked tirelessly to figure out a solution. He had the office reorganized, assembled a brand new team of specialists, and carried out a complete strategy revision. It wasn't a piece of cake — there were days when even he felt embarrassed by how far the company had fallen — but step by step, things started to work out.

When BrightPath finally made it back to profitability, the CEO called a meeting. "This team deserves recognition," she said. "Whatever challenges came our way, you supported each other and never stopped improving." Lucas blushed — he was never used to receiving praise. But deep inside, he knew they all had a gift for making things work, even when times were tough.
