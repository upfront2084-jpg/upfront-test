Dialogue — A Medical Emergency Abroad

Traveler: My travel companion is in critical condition, and the hospital says they need authorization from our insurance before starting treatment. What should I do?

Insurance Agent: I understand the urgency. Please give the hospital our insurance hotline number — we can authorize emergency treatment immediately over the phone.

Traveler: Thank you. Also, given how serious this is, is a medical evacuation back to Brazil something we should consider?

Insurance Agent: Let's wait for the doctor's assessment first, but yes, if needed, our policy covers medical evacuation in life-threatening situations.

Resolving a Travel Problem
