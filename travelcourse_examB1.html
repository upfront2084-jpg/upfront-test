Dialogue — Resolving a Team Conflict

Camila: Thiago, I've received a grievance from two employees who work on your team.

Thiago: I heard about it. Ana and Bruno, who both handle client accounts, have been in conflict for weeks.

Camila: That's right. Ana told me that Bruno often interrupts her during meetings.

Thiago: I see. If we don't address this soon, it will affect the whole team's morale.

Camila: Agreed. We should schedule a mediation session this week.

Thiago: I can facilitate it myself, or we could bring in someone from Employee Relations.

Camila: Actually, this case should be handled by someone who is neutral, so Employee Relations should lead it.

Thiago: That makes sense. What's the next step?

Camila: I'll set up the meeting and make sure everyone understands the confidentiality policy.

Thiago: Perfect. I'm confident we can find a resolution that works for both of them.

Roteiro (apenas para o professor ler em voz alta)

This is a short case study on workplace mediation, Case File 118-C. Six weeks ago, an employee named Marcus filed a formal complaint about unfair shift scheduling in the warehouse team. The complaint reached the regional HR office within two days, and an investigator named Elena Cruz was assigned to review it. Elena interviewed five colleagues and checked the schedule records, keeping every detail private throughout the process. After three weeks, Marcus and his supervisor agreed to sit down together with a neutral facilitator. The meeting lasted ninety minutes, and by the end, they had built a new rotation system that both sides accepted. Morale in the warehouse team improved noticeably afterward, and no further complaints were filed that quarter. This case shows how quick action and confidentiality can rebuild trust.
